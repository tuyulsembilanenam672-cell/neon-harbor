Neon Harbor — an original open-world crime game set in Port Solmar: explore a neon harbour city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the harbour police under a 5-star wanted system, fight the Rust Vipers, and finish fixer Marla Voss's three jobs to earn your way out.

## Play it
```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
No build step, no network requests — everything (including the vendored three.js build) is a static file under `game/`.

## How to play
- **WASD** move / drive · **E** steal & enter a car · **F** punch
- **Shift** sprint · **Space** brake · **drag mouse** to look around
- **P** pause · **C** save · **M** mute

Follow the yellow beacon to each job: a harbor courier run, a casino getaway with the police on your tail, and a turf war at the docks. Crimes raise your wanted level; lay low and out of sight to cool it off. Progress saves to localStorage — press C any time.

## Features
- A full 3D city (Port Solmar) with districts, traffic, pedestrians, a harbour and a sunset sky
- Driving and on-foot play with a third-person camera
- Car stealing, traffic, taxis and police pursuit with a 5-star wanted level
- Three story missions with briefings, timers and cash rewards
- Combat, health, busted / wasted game-over screens
- HUD with money, health, wanted stars, district, mission tracker and a live minimap
- WebAudio sound: engine hum, police sirens, punches and mission jingles
- Save / continue via localStorage
